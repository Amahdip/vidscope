How to read it

Severity follows the source

MUST
A requirement (also SHALL, SHALL NOT). Breaking it is critical.
SHOULD · Recommends
A recommendation. Breaking it is a warning, unless the rule explains otherwise.
MAY · Defines · Adapted
An option, a definition with no requirement, or a requirement the audit adapts to a single file. Reported as information, or with a severity that is our own judgement and says so.
Practice · Service
Not a standard: industry practice, or the service's own profile.
