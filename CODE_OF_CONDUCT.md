# Code of conduct

Vidscope follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and keep discussion about the
work. Harassment, insults and personal attacks are not accepted anywhere in the project's
spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews.

Report unacceptable behaviour privately to the maintainer through
[GitHub](https://github.com/Amahdip). Reports are handled confidentially, and the maintainer may
remove comments, lock threads or block people who break the code of conduct.
